# Editorial Travel Collage Style

Use this style for `editorial_collage` scenes. Carry its clear information hierarchy, pacing, and restrained palette through the overall edit; do not add paper textures, fake cut-outs, or kinetic typography inside `real_life_documentary` footage.

Keep one coherent collage style block across the graphic scenes:

- mixed-media hand-cut paper collage
- torn/scissor-cut edges
- tape corners and paper drop shadows
- halftone/newsprint texture
- bold flat 2–3 color palette per scene
- clean infographic hierarchy
- layered foreground/midground/background paper pieces
- straight-on editorial framing
- large short headline, small utility labels
- rigid paper motion: slide, flutter, pivot, drift, parallax, settle
- avoid glossy 3D CGI and photoreal-render drift

For motion use one camera move per scene: slow push-in, pull-out, pan, tilt, subtle parallax, or locked-off static. Avoid adjacent repetition unless a route/timeline intentionally uses it.
